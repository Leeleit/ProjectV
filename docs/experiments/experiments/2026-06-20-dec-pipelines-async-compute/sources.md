## 7. Sources explicitly NOT used (with reason)

- **Older (pre-2024) async-compute blog posts** — Vulkan 1.3 (with sync2 core) + Vulkan 1.4 (with global
  priority core) changed the landscape significantly. Pre-2024 advice often ignores timeline semaphores
  or recommends `VK_SHARING_MODE_CONCURRENT` (which Lou Kramer 2019 already warned against for production).
- **D3D12-specific async-compute** — ProjectV is Vulkan. D3D12 multi-queue UAV overlapping is explicitly
  forbidden by Epic (per Nanite forums 2025-04-25), but Vulkan is more permissive. Don't conflate.
- **WebGL/WebGPU** — not relevant (ProjectV = C++26 + Vulkan 1.3 per TODO §A1).
- **Pre-2020 papers on persistent compute** — superseded by 2024-2026 work (Leapfrog Flow Maps 2025,
  FluidFormer 2025, PICT 2025).
- **Vendor white papers for unreleased hardware** (e.g. RTX 60-series rumors) — not yet available.
