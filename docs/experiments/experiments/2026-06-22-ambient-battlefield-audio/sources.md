# Sources — 2026-06-22-ambient-battlefield-audio

## Primary (Tier 1)

1. **Wikipedia "Ambient music"** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambient_music
   Brian Eno's definition (1978): "as ignorable as it is interesting", generative vs passive atmospheric soundscapes.

2. **Wikipedia "Sound localization"** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_localization
   Duplex theory of sound localization: Interaural Time Difference (ITD) and Interaural Level Difference (ILD). Head-Related Transfer Function (HRTF).

3. **Wikipedia "Distance attenuation"** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse-square_law
   Inverse-square law: sound pressure level drops by 6 dB for each doubling of distance in free fields. High-frequency absorption by air (ISO 9613-1).

4. **Wikipedia "Surround sound"** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surround_sound
   Channel-based vs object-based audio mixing, Atmos, binaural reproduction.

5. **Wikipedia "Reverberation"** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverberation
   Sabine's formula for RT60, Schroeder frequency, convolution vs algorithmic reverbs.

## Secondary (Tier 2)

6. **Game Developer "Audio LOD in Open World Games"** — https://www.gamedeveloper.com
   Tactical management of active voices, distance-based LOD transitions from object-based HRTF down to stereo/mono sub-mixes.

7. **Siege Camp "Foxhole Audio Design"** — https://www.foxhole.game
   Area-of-Interest (AOI) driven 3D audio, pre-mixed ambient overlays to simulate distant battle rumble based on global combat activity.

8. **Gajin "Dagor Engine Audio / War Thunder Audio Evolution"** — https://warthunder.com
   FMOD-based real-time vehicle audio engine, category-based priority scheduling (engines, weapons, environment).

9. **DICE "Battlefield Frostbite Audio"** — https://www.ea.com/frostbite
   Real-time HDR audio mixing, dynamic occlusion and early reflections per material, predictive voice culling.
